Bhoomi Folk Festival 2023: official aftermovie

Cinematography · Editing · Janakpur, Nepal

The first edition of Bhoomi Folk Festival, cut into a film. It moves from the road into Janakpur to a street vendor at her cart, then into the festival: dholak players, dancers in red-and-white costume on the sand, and a crowd pressed against the barricades.

After dark the film shifts to stage light and packed audiences, and closes on the sponsor roll. The full film runs a little over two minutes.

19th Global Film Festival Noida: Day 2

Event coverage · AAFT, Noida · 1080p source

A run through the second day of the festival at AAFT. A brass band plays at the entrance, the festival's film-reel installation greets arrivals, and a photo exhibition draws visitors in.

Inside, the coverage moves to the lit stage: dignitaries at the podium, a live conga performance and the crowd between sessions. Handheld and gimbal-style coverage that keeps pace with a busy venue.

Global Film Festival Noida: Day 2 highlights

Highlights edit · AAFT, Noida · 1080p source

A tighter highlights edit of the same day. It follows the festival's key moments: the welcome on stage, the panel conversation, a ribbon-cutting at the exhibition and a student vocal performance.

Wide frames of the hall alternate with close portraits of speakers and performers. Built to give organisers a shareable recap the same week.
